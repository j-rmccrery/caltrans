Is a pass right, and how much is checked

Two questions a pass rate cannot answer. Precision: every checked label on R-10434.2 and R-10434.3 was rendered with the line the checker measured and keyed by eye; the bench re-scores each run against those keys. Coverage: every distance or bearing token on the sheet is attributed; the denominator is the labels a check could address (table cells, notes, areas and radial bearings excluded, each counted in its own bucket).

Fails are now claims about the record. A fail says the record disagrees with the drawing, so a result far beyond any real disagreement in the keyed sets (bearing over 3°, distance over 400 ft or 200 %, radius over 110 %, arc over 175 ft and 90 %) is queued as “wrong line likely”. Tags resolved from the record and bare (T) totals may only add passes. Every keyed real fail is kept. The cuts come from the two keyed sheets, and most wrong-line arc fails on R-10434.3 remain because no safe arc cut exists.
